# Atelier Dimension

Design read: A fashion audience should feel they have stepped inside a working image, where garments, tape and geometry occupy real depth instead of sitting behind a webpage.

Concept spine: The measuring tape becomes the camera path through a physical fashion collage.

Delivery tier: spectacle

Locked palette: #EEF1F0 porcelain, #151716 carbon, #8E1747 berry, #F3D000 tape yellow. Cool neutrals keep the UI architectural while berry and tape yellow pull directly from the garments and measuring tape.

Locked type: Instrument Sans for the structural display voice, Georgia italic for the couture counterpoint, IBM Plex Mono for technical labels.

Animation mode: animated-website
Journey shape: single-shot. One continuous source-based fashion film carries the main scroll journey while a layered WebGL scene creates a second depth beat above it.

Journey: Establish the complete garment composition. Move into the central burgundy form. Travel across geometric detail. Pass the measuring tape. Resolve on the full atelier composition.

World grammar: cool porcelain interface, carbon film stage, berry garment accents, saturated tape yellow, crisp studio photography, real textile texture, soft physical shadows, restrained glass, no abstract spheres.

Mobile framing: keep the large pink mannequin, burgundy garment and tape center-safe; crop the outer background before the garments; reduce lateral layer travel.

Section plan: layered opening, world composition, scroll film, material laboratory, construction studies, signature reveal, footer.

Asset plan: source-based hero image, source image depth layers, generated source-based scroll film with desktop and mobile encodes and exact posters, detail motion clip with desktop and mobile encodes and posters, monogram, favicon set and metadata card.

CTA inventory: Enter the study, Start a conversation. Each uses a distinct treatment and is not shared as a global button component.

Signature interaction: pointer and scroll alter depth in the opening scene while scroll directly controls the source-based film.

Anti-convergence note: this replaces the previous cream-and-charcoal glass page with a cooler porcelain/carbon/berry system, a source-image depth rig, a real garment film, a construction crop system and a bespoke cursor.
